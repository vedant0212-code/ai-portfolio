SAMPLE PROPERTY DAMAGE POLICY (illustrative wording written for this project; not a real insurance contract)

1.1 Covered Property
The policy covers furniture, fixtures, fittings, flooring, walls, ceilings, linens and appliances located inside the insured hotel, motel or short-term rental unit.

2.1 Accidental Water Damage
The policy covers damage caused by the sudden and accidental discharge, leakage or overflow of water from plumbing, heating systems, appliances, taps or sinks. Burst pipes and flooding that occur suddenly are included.

2.2 Fire, Smoke and Burn Damage
The policy covers damage from fire, smoke, soot, scorching and burns, including burns caused by cigarettes, candles or cooking.

2.3 Guest Damage
The policy covers accidental or intentional damage to furniture, fixtures and fittings caused by a guest, including breakage, tears, spills, stains and impact damage to doors, frames and walls.

2.4 Theft and Missing Items
The policy covers theft of insured property by a guest or visitor. Items reported missing at check-out are covered only when a police report has been filed.

2.5 Vandalism by Third Parties
The policy covers malicious damage by persons who are not guests, including graffiti, smashed windows and forced entry.

3.1 Wear and Tear Exclusion
The policy does not cover wear and tear, fading, ageing, deterioration from normal use or damage that builds up over years of ordinary use.

3.2 Gradual Damage Exclusion
The policy does not cover mould, damp, condensation, seepage or any damage that develops gradually or over time.

3.3 Pre-existing Damage Exclusion
The policy does not cover damage that existed before the incident, including damage noted in earlier inspections or reports.

4.1 Evidence Requirements
Every claim must include dated photographs of the damage. Water damage claims must include a plumber or maintenance report on the cause. Fire and smoke claims must include an incident or fire service report. Theft claims must include a police report.

4.2 Claim Deadline
Claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident.

4.3 Deductible
A deductible applies to each claim and is deducted from the assessed amount.
